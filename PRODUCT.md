# Product

## Register

product

## Users
The primary user is the owner of the game accounts, using the app as a private planning tool to track platinum trophies and 100% completions across platforms. The app is used between work, studies, and limited gaming sessions, so it needs to make priorities, monthly backlog, guide links, notes, and progress easy to review quickly.

## Product Purpose
Zenkuri organizes trophy and achievement progress across Steam, PlayStation Network, and RetroAchievements. It should help decide what to play next, track what is already completed, preserve useful context for each game, and make the personal completion history satisfying to browse.

## Brand Personality
Stylish, gamer, intelligent. The interface should feel polished and game-aware without falling into generic AI cyberpunk decoration. It should be pleasant to look at while staying practical for repeated planning.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic corporate dashboards, marketing/landing-page layouts, excessive neon cyberpunk defaults, decorative clutter, and interfaces that look impressive but make planning slower.

## Design Principles
1. Planning first: every screen should reduce the effort of choosing what to play next.
2. Progress should feel rewarding: completed games and active platinum hunts should be easy and satisfying to inspect.
3. Multi-platform by design: Steam, PSN, and RetroAchievements should feel like equal sources within one personal system.
4. Useful density: show enough information for decisions without turning the app into a spreadsheet.
5. Style supports focus: visual personality should make the tool enjoyable, not noisy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target readable contrast, clear keyboard focus, familiar controls, and reduced-motion friendly transitions. Color should reinforce state but not be the only way to understand progress, priority, or completion.
